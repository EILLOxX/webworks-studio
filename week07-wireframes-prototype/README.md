## 5. Prototype Flow

Describe the user task your prototype demonstrates.

**Starting point:** it shows that all buttons and links are clickable.
**User action:** by pressing on the catagoery buttons and pressing the header links.
**System/interface response:** catagoery button sorts the trails with a specific label and the navagation links takes you to certain sections of the page.
**End state:** it shows only the trail cards you filtered for, and the navagaton link takes you to diffrent page sections.

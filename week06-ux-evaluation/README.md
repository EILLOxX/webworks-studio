## Hill Country Trail Guide

**Primary User:** Maya Torres  
**Primary Task:** Choose a beginner-appropriate Saturday hike that can be completed in about three hours or less.

---

## 1. Task Walkthrough

Briefly describe what Maya would try to do first, what she would look for, and where she might hesitate.

while on phone, she would scan the page and look at the pictures first, she would look for an easy trial that's dog friendly and short. she might hesitate when it comes to reading the trails info since some of the trails info doesnt include proper trail classification thats easy to read.

---

## 2. Five UX Findings

Document **exactly five meaningful findings**.

### Finding 1

**Observation:**  
trail classification unclear

**Evidence:**  
it appears to be important info unorganized in clutter text

**User Impact:**  
it would prevent her from quickly seeing what is the safest or easist trail and what is allowed etc.

**Principle:**  
Hierarchy and consistency principle

**Priority:**
high

**Recommendation:**  
having important info be more easier to read

### Finding 2

**Observation:**  
there's no pictures showing what anything is about

**Evidence:**
it appears missing

**User Impact:**
it wouldn't let her view and see what the text is talking about visually

**Principle:**
cognitive load principle

**Priority:**
low

**Recommendation:**  
having more visual clarity to see what she will be getting into

### Finding 3

**Observation:**
there's no estemation of how long each trails would take

**Evidence:**
fails to show any visual marker of the time it would take to complete a trail

**User Impact:**
prevents her from quickly finding a trail by the estemated completion time

**Principle:**
acessibility princible

**Priority:**
medium

**Recommendation:**
something to show the time to comeplete a trail walk

### Finding 4

**Observation:**
no past reviews

**Evidence:**
missing reviw section

**User Impact:**
this makes maya unable to read a trail about other people's time there

**Principle:**
feedback

**Priority:**
low

**Recommendation:**
something that allows people to add their experience and their tips.

### Finding 5

**Observation:**
main links dont function as they should

**Evidence:**
links dont work when clicked or show better link accessibility

**User Impact:**
this effects mayas abilitly of learning more about trails and whats best for her

**Principle:**
navagation and accessiblity princible

**Priority:**
medium

**Recommendation:**
have something that the navagations link to and shows that the action can be done.
